About this model ＋

Real elevation data, sampled on a 31 m grid over an 8 × 8 km area. Drainage uses depression filling and steepest-descent (D8) routing. Paths can cross filled depressions; they represent potential drainage, not observed streams or a flood forecast. Soil, rainfall, culverts and buildings are not modeled. Paths end at the map boundary; upstream areas outside the map are excluded. Animation speed is illustrative.

Terrain wetness estimates the topographic wetness index (TWI) from slope and contributing area. Higher values indicate greater potential for water accumulation, not a measured moisture percentage. Colors are scaled to this study area. Flood scenarios spread from one chosen source across connected terrain below a held water surface, using the original elevations. Flood statistics stop at the study boundary. The 31 m grid does not resolve small barriers or channels.

Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles. Europe terrain produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union — EU-DEM layers. Global SRTM / GMTED2010 data courtesy of USGS. Data credits ↗
